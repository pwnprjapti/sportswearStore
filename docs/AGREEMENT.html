2 Scope of Work & Deliverables
The Agency agrees to design, engineer, and deploy a custom activewear e-commerce website with the following specific features:
  • Cultstore-Style UI & UX: Clean, minimalist landing page with an auto-sliding hero banner (4 active slides, mobile touch swipe, navigation arrows & indicator dots) and horizontal sliding product rails ("Trending in Men's Sports" & "Popular in Kids' Sports").
  • Men's & Children's Sports Wear Catalog: Dedicated sections for Gym Tees, Trackpants, Shorts, Kids Football Sets, Tracksuits, and PE wear with size and subcategory filters.
  • Razorpay Online Payment Gateway: Full integration of Razorpay secure checkout supporting UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking & Wallets with instant payment confirmation and automated order creation. (Note: Standard Razorpay transaction fee of ~2% + GST is charged directly by Razorpay per sale; Client shall provide Razorpay Merchant KYC API keys).
  • Multi-Channel Ordering Options: Razorpay Online Payment + 1-Click WhatsApp Direct Ordering + Cash on Delivery (COD) mode.
  • Store Owner Admin Dashboard (/admin): Secure credential-protected portal to add, edit, or remove products, manage pricing, view received orders, update delivery status, and click-to-chat directly with customers on WhatsApp.
  • Order Tracking: Live status tracker for customers using their Order ID or Phone Number.
  • Domain & Cloud Deployment: 1-Year Domain Registration (.in / .com) and high-speed Node.js cloud deployment with free SSL security certificate (zero monthly platform fees).
  • Catalog Provisioning: Initial upload and formatting of up to 15 products provided by the Client.
