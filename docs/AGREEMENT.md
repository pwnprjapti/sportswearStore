### **1. Scope of Work & Deliverables**
The Agency agrees to design, build, test, and deploy a custom activewear e-commerce website with the following specifications:
1. **Cultstore-Style Sliding UI/UX:**
   - Auto-sliding hero banner (4 active athletic slides, mobile touch swipe, pause-on-hover, navigation arrows & dot indicators).
   - Horizontal sliding product carousels ("🔥 Trending in Men's Sports" & "⚽ Popular in Kids' Sports") with snap scrolling.
   - Circular category story strip (*T-shirts, Joggers, Shorts, Kids Kits, Tracksuits, School PE*).
2. **Catalog & Filters:** Full activewear catalog with dedicated Men's and Kids' sections, activity filter chips, size picker (`M`, `L`, `XL`, `4-5Y`, etc.), and sorting.
3. **Razorpay Online Payment Gateway:** Full integration of Razorpay secure checkout supporting UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking & Wallets with real-time payment confirmation and automated order creation. *(Note: Standard Razorpay transaction fee of ~2% + GST is charged directly by Razorpay per sale; Client shall provide Razorpay Merchant KYC API keys).*
4. **Multi-Channel Ordering:** Instant Razorpay Online Checkout + 1-Click WhatsApp Direct Ordering + Cash on Delivery (COD) mode.
5. **Cart Drawer & Free Shipping Tracker:** Slide-out drawer with dynamic progress bar for free shipping threshold (₹799).
6. **Live Order Tracking:** Real-time customer tracking portal by Order ID or phone number.
7. **Store Owner Admin Panel (`/admin`):** Secure dashboard to add/edit/delete products, manage prices, view incoming orders, update shipping status, and click-to-chat directly with customers on WhatsApp.
8. **Domain & Cloud Infrastructure:** 1-Year Domain Registration (.in / .com) and Node.js cloud server configuration with free SSL certificate (Zero monthly platform fees).
9. **Initial Catalog Provisioning:** Upload and formatting of up to 15 products provided by the Client.
